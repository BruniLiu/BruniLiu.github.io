About

Xiangyi (Bruni) Liu is an undergraduate at Liaoning University. Her research asks how 3D space should be parameterized and represented. This question has run through her work from 3D Gaussian Splatting, to sparse-view CT reconstruction, where she treats reconstruction as an ill-posed inverse problem, and now to memory and 3D structure in world models.

She prefers mathematical solutions over heuristics. She has been a visiting student at Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology (SUAT) and University College London (UCL), and has interned at Harbin Institute of Technology.

  • 2025.10–04

    Budgeted Topology Formation for Compact 3D Gaussian Splatting

    FM-TopoFreeze · Liaoning University

    Treats 3DGS densification as budget-constrained topology formation during training. Feasibility-matched candidate selection, driven by image residuals, raises target-budget realization from 92.0% to 100.0%. On Mip-NeRF 360 it improves PSNR on 7/7 scenes over a fixed-topology baseline (average 28.503 → 28.687 dB).
